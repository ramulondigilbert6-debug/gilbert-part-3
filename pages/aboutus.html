About Bright Spark Electrical

Powering homes, businesses, and communities in Polokwane with trusted craftsmanship and modern solar innovations.

Our Story & Mission

Founded with a passion for excellence and safety, Bright Spark Electrical has grown into a leading electrical contractor in Polokwane and the broader Limpopo region. We specialize in residential maintenance, commercial fault finding, and sustainable hybrid solar power installations.

Our mission is simple: provide reliable energy solutions, maintain strict compliance with SANS 10142 regulations, and ensure every property we serve is safe, power-efficient, and resilient against load shedding.

Our Core Values
